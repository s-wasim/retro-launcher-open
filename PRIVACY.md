# Privacy Policy — Minimal Retro Launcher

_Effective: **[DATE]** · Contact: **[YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]**_

Minimal Retro Launcher ("the app") is a home-screen launcher for Android. It has
no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. This page lists
everything the app does with your data.

## What leaves your phone

**Your location, for weather — only if you allow it.** When you grant location
permission, the app sends your coordinates (to about 11 metres) to
[Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms#privacy) to fetch the local
weather. That is the only data the app sends anywhere. The request carries no
identifier for you or your device. The app keeps the last reading on your phone
so it can show it at start-up. Deny the permission and the app shows no live
weather; everything else still works.

## What stays on your phone

- **Screen time (usage access).** If you grant it, the app reads how long you
  used your apps today to show totals and compare them with your daily limit.
  This is processed on your device and never sent anywhere.
- **Accessibility service (optional).** If you turn it on, it is used only to
  lock the screen when you double-tap home and to open the notification shade
  when you swipe down. It does not read your screen, notifications or anything
  you type.
- **Your settings, app list and icons** are stored in the app's private
  storage and are deleted when you uninstall it.

## Sharing, selling, children

The app does not sell or share personal data. It is not directed at children
under 13.
